Portfolio stress test

What would a shock cost this portfolio?

Load the list of holdings from a fund report or a bank export. You get yield, duration and the value of one basis point, then five stress scenarios and one of your own, holding by holding.

The file stays in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.

Drop a CSV file here. Comma or semicolon, German or English number format.

The example: 21 holdings from page 6 of the BayernInvest Lux Multi Asset Income semi-annual report as of 31 March 2026 (18 bonds, gold, two fund units).

Needed: a name or ISIN, and the market value. Better: holding, price, maturity and coupon for bonds. Optional: type, duration or beta per holding. Download the template

        How it is calculated

        Bonds: the yield is solved from the clean price plus accrued interest, over the bond's own coupon dates (yearly, half-yearly for Italian BTPs). Duration and convexity come from moving that yield one basis point either way.

        A scenario moves German yields, a spread for each group of issuer countries, an extra spread for covered and other bonds, expected inflation, equities and gold. A bond's price changes by minus duration times the yield move, plus half the convexity times its square. Inflation-linked bonds move with real yields, so higher expected inflation softens their loss. Each part of the result moves one factor at a time, so the parts add up.

        Funds and shares: equity funds move with equities (beta 1 unless the file sets another), bond funds with a duration the file can set (otherwise 4 years, or 1 for reserve and money-market funds). Gold moves with gold. Currencies, defaults and accrued interest are left out.

        The scenarios are stated round numbers, not forecasts or replays of history. Prototype for discussion, not investment advice.
